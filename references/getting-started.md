# Getting Started

> Discord Developers reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

The Discord Social SDK brings Discord social capabilities into a game. It lets players interact with friends and Discord communities from inside the game instead of switching away. If you are new to the Discord Social SDK, start with the Discord Social SDK [Overview](https://docs.discord.com/developers/discord-social-sdk/overview) and [Core Concepts](https://docs.discord.com/developers/discord-social-sdk/core-concepts) to understand the SDK's capabilities and why they may help your game.
