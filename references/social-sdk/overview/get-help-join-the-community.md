### Get Help & Join the Community

 Use this section when you need assistance or want contact with other game developers.
- Join the [DDevs Discord Server](https://discord.gg/discord-developers) for community help, shared best practices, and ways to improve your game. The relevant channel is `#social-sdk-dev-help`.
- For more support, [file a ticket with Developer Support](https://dis.gd/social-sdk) when reporting issues.
