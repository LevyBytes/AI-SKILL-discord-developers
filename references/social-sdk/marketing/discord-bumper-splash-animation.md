- Best Practice Guidance for Discord Social SDK
  - Use this as an intro splash for the game, an SDK launch announcement video, or trailer content that actually shows Discord Social SDK features. Do not include the splash video in content that does not present Discord Social SDK features.

- Usage Guidance
  - Place the splash animation at the start of the video. Do not insert it in the middle or at the end.
  - Discord prefers the 3 second video, although 1 second and 2 second variants are also available.
