### Overview

 Your game now uses Discord Social SDK for social features, so the next step is telling players what changed. This toolkit gives launch guidance, communication practices, FAQ-oriented messaging, and brand-use constraints so you can explain the integration clearly and follow Discord brand rules. Marketing the integration to players remains your responsibility and choice. Do not imply a Discord partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement unless Discord has given prior written approval for that specific statement.
