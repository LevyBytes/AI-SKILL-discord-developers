### Legal Brand Guidelines

 DISCORD, DISCORD NITRO, the "Clyde" Logo, other trademarks owned by Discord and its affiliates (the "Discord Marks"), and brand materials including logos, trade dress, Discord look and feel, and other distinctive aesthetic brand elements (the "Brand Assets") belong exclusively to Discord Inc. You need Discord's permission before using Discord Marks or Brand Assets except where this document allows that use. See the [Discord Brand Guidelines](https://discord.com/branding) for additional legal rules that govern use of Discord's Brand Assets.
