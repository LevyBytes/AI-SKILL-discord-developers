### Integration Announcement Plan Recommendations

 The following channels are recommended for announcing a Discord Social SDK integration to players. Use additional channels as needed for the audiences your players actually follow.
- **In-Game Messaging or Reward (Best)**
  - Use a pop-up, banner, or CTA to announce the update, ask players to link a Discord account, and optionally attach a reward.

- **Announcement Video (Best)**
  - Demonstrate the integration and its benefits with video.

- **Individual Blog Post**
  - Publish a blog post with a fuller explanation of the integration.

- **Patch Notes**
  - Treat the Discord integration details and images as a major update in patch notes.

- **Social Media**
  - Announce through other social channels where players follow updates.

- **Discord Server Announcement**
  - Post in your main Discord announcements channel so the community knows about the update. Add a dedicated discussion area for feedback.

- **Creator Partnership**
  - Work with a creator who can show the integration to their audience.

- **Paid Media**
  - Use paid advertising to prompt Discord account linking.

- **Press**
  - Share the integration story with relevant press. For press questions, include [press@discord.com](mailto:press@discord.com).

- **Support Ticket Setup**
  - Add a support ticket category for related player issues, for example a "Discord account linking" category.
