### Discord Brand Assets & Guidance

 Discord appreciates developers helping promote Discord. Use the correct assets and follow brand-team guidance. Any use of Discord Marks and Brand Assets must comply with Discord's [Brand Guidelines found here](https://discord.com/branding). Do not state or imply partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement by Discord unless Discord has given prior written approval for that exact use.
