### Key Benefits to Players

- **Find your friends**
  - Link your Discord account so you can see friends who are playing and join them more easily. Invite friends from inside the game, including friends who are currently offline.

- **Stay connected**
  - Continue conversations by syncing messages and friend lists with Discord.
