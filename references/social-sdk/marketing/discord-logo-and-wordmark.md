### Discord Logo and Wordmark

- [Download Link](https://discord.com/branding)
- Guidance for Discord Social SDK
  - Before using the logo or wordmark in in-game CTA buttons, social images, or blog visuals, review the Logo & Symbol sections of the brand guidelines. Direct uncertainty or usage questions to your Discord point of contact.
  - For in-game messaging direction, consult the articles on [Connection Points](https://docs.discord.com/developers/discord-social-sdk/design-guidelines/connection-points) and [Overall Flow](https://docs.discord.com/developers/discord-social-sdk/design-guidelines/signing-in#before-the-user-connects).
