### A Note on the Product Name

 The formal product name is "Discord Social SDK". For player announcements, Discord recommends simpler, more conversational wording, and most player-facing copy does not need to include the full product name. Use the guidance below for messaging that tends to work well with players. If the full "Discord Social SDK" name is used, subsequent mentions in the same announcement may shorten it to the "SDK".
