# Forum Channels Release

Date: 2022-09-14
[Official source](https://github.com/discord/discord-api-docs/blob/37148620d2324292b76047541d5f8331f81ea77a/developers/change-log.mdx#L3654) · [Year index](INDEX.md)

<a id="source-forum-channels-release"></a>


Forum channels ([`GUILD_FORUM` or `15`](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/channel#channel-object-channel-types)) are available to all community servers. `GUILD_FORUM` is a channel type that supports only threads, displayed differently than text (`GUILD_TEXT`) channels. See the [forums topic](https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/threads#forums) for APIs and technical details, and the [Forums FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/6208479917079-Forum-Channels-FAQ#h_01G69FJQWTWN88HFEHK7Z6X79N) for feature details.
