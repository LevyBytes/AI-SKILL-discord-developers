Discord published a guide for the new [invite endpoints](https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log#new-invite-endpoints) and related features. It shows how to create role-granting invites through the UI and API, and how to limit invite access with `target_users` through the API. The guide focuses on community-server use cases and explains how to create and send invites that assign roles or restrict acceptance to selected users. [Check out the new guide here!](https://docs.discord.com/developers/tutorials/using-community-invites)
