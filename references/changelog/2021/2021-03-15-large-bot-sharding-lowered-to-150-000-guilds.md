# Large Bot Sharding Lowered to 150,000 Guilds

Date: 2021-03-15
[Official source](https://github.com/discord/discord-api-docs/blob/37148620d2324292b76047541d5f8331f81ea77a/developers/change-log.mdx#L4055) · [Year index](INDEX.md)

<a id="source-large-bot-sharding-lowered-to-150000-guilds"></a>


Bots in many guilds were reported to hit more session-start errors, including gateway connection timeouts. To improve reliability, the large bot sharding threshold was lowered to 150,000 guilds.
