# Premium App Subscriptions Now Available in the EU and UK

Date: 2023-10-19
Categories: Premium Apps
[Official source](https://github.com/discord/discord-api-docs/blob/37148620d2324292b76047541d5f8331f81ea77a/developers/change-log.mdx#L3131) · [Year index](INDEX.md)

<a id="source-premium-app-subscriptions-now-available-in-the-eu-and-uk"></a>


Starting today, eligible EU and UK developers can monetize verified apps with App Subscriptions. [App Subscriptions](https://docs.discord.com/developers/monetization/overview) let apps charge users for premium functionality through a recurring, monthly subscription. New Premium App Subscriptions features are documented in the [App Subscriptions overview](https://docs.discord.com/developers/monetization/overview) and [the changelog for the previous App Subscriptions release](https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log#premium-app-subscriptions-available-in-the-us). To learn eligibility and enablement details, see the [Monetization Overview](https://docs.discord.com/developers/monetization/overview).
