# Deprecation: Accept Invite Endpoint

Date: 2018-01-23
[Official source](https://github.com/discord/discord-api-docs/blob/37148620d2324292b76047541d5f8331f81ea77a/developers/change-log.mdx#L4437) · [Year index](INDEX.md)

<a id="source-deprecation-accept-invite-endpoint"></a>


[Accept Invite](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/invite) is deprecated as of today and will be removed on March 23, 2018. Use [Add Guild Member](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild#add-guild-member) instead.
